WHY WE STARTED

Because we've been
the students too.

We know what it's like to have five tabs open, three different revision sites, a deadline tomorrow and no idea where to begin.

StudyGroup is being built by students who wanted a clearer, calmer way to learn. We are making the kind of study space we wished we had: useful, welcoming and shaped by the people who actually use it.
